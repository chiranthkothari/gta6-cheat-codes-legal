Privacy Policy

Cheat Codes for GTA 6 · Effective date: November 12, 2026

This app is an unofficial fan companion. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Microsoft or Sony.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your selected platform, theme, haptics preference, Follow mode settings and disclaimer acknowledgement.
  • Your favorites and recent searches.
  • The time your ad-free session ends.
  • The bundled code database, which works offline.

This information stays on your device. The app has no account system and does not send this information to a server operated by the app.

Ads and consent

The app uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show ads and to collect advertising consent where the law requires it. Google and its advertising partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic data under their own privacy policies. The app asks for consent before personalized ads where required and provides a Privacy and ad choices entry while consent choices are available.

  • Google privacy policy
  • Google ad technology

The app does not contain third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs beyond Google Mobile Ads and UMP.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only for ads, consent and app-update links. Codes and favorites work without a connection. The code database is bundled with the app and is not downloaded from a remote service.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Ads are configured through Google AdMob and UMP; do not use the app if you are below the age required by your local law.

Data deletion

You can remove favorites and recent searches inside the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all locally stored app data. Ad consent choices can be changed from Settings > Privacy and ad choices while that option is available.

Changes

This policy may change when the app changes. The effective date above will be updated. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
